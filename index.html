Flagship · Caterpillar — Construction Industries Services Division

Validating a new aftermarket service line in 30 days

Caterpillar's Construction Industries Services Division (CISD) — the group focused on accelerating aftermarket services growth — wanted to know whether small-core operators would choose an L3 hydraulic-pump repair service over a Reman exchange. We ran the whole test as a self-contained lane, in parallel to their normal process.

The opportunity

A division focused on growing aftermarket services — looking for a way to test new service concepts at market speed, without tying up the internal review cycles built for full product launches.

What we did

Stood up Hydraline Pro — a fully branded concept site offering repair pricing at 15–55% of new-pump cost, with warranty, financing, and expedited-service options. Targeted real operators via a first-party data partner plus PMAX and Meta. Instrumented every step with custom GA4 events — no PII collected.

The outcome

Statistically significant validation of both demand and pricing sensitivity — delivered as a final report plus a live Looker Studio dashboard. Caterpillar greenlit a dealership pilot on the strength of it.
